STAGE 1 RESEARCH CONTRIBUTION · LITERATURE CATALOGING (HUIT 2024–2025)
STAGE 1 LITERATURE LOG · PRIOR TO IT TRANSFER AT LHU

Faculty Research Topic: Winter Tourism Consumer Behavior in Da Nang City

Actual Role: Searching related academic papers (Google Scholar & Perplexity AI), screening abstracts & cataloging literature into a structured Excel sheet

During my Hospitality studies at HUIT, I participated in the initial literature discovery phase of a faculty-supervised research project. My responsibility was to search for academic papers and reports related to off-peak tourism seasonality and winter tourist psychology, screen their abstracts, and systematically log them into a structured Excel spreadsheet before I decided to transfer schools and majors to study IT at LHU.

Stage-1 Literature Search & Excel Cataloging Workflow
  • 1. Targeted Paper Retrieval: Combined keyword queries on Google Scholar with Perplexity AI to quickly locate relevant studies and reports on seasonal tourism consumer behavior and weather sensitivity.
  • 2. Abstract Screening & Relevance Filtering: Read through paper abstracts and conclusions to verify relevance to off-peak/winter coastal tourism prior to cataloging.
  • 3. Structured Excel Literature Log: Compiled and organized all discovered papers into an Excel spreadsheet (tracking Paper Title, Authors/Year, Source Link, Abstract Summary, and Relevance Notes) as a foundational reference sheet for the research team.

The Academic Pivot to Computer Science: While completing this Stage-1 Excel literature catalog at HUIT, experiencing firsthand how AI discovery tools accelerate scientific synthesis sparked a defining realization: I wanted to build the technology itself. Consequently, before this tourism study concluded, I made the decisive transition from HUIT Hospitality to Information Technology at Lac Hong University (LHU).
