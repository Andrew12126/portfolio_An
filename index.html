The Origin & Academic Pivot Story

I began my university education with 3 semesters of Hospitality Management at HUIT (2024 – 2025). This formative period instilled in me a core engineering philosophy: always understand human psychology deeply, respect every service touchpoint, and prioritize human comfort and peace of mind above all else.

During my time at HUIT, while participating in the initial stage of a faculty-supervised research project — searching for related academic papers, reading abstracts, and cataloging literature systematically into an Excel database using AI discovery tools — I realized my true calling was to directly engineer intelligent software systems. Before that research project concluded, I made the decisive pivot to transfer schools and majors into Information Technology (IT) at Lac Hong University (LHU), where I am currently a first-year undergraduate.

Alongside building rigorous foundations in Discrete Mathematics, Algorithms, and Programming at LHU, my experience hosting 4–5 community and cultural events (charity drives, Mid-Autumn festivals, Dong Ho folk art exhibitions, minigames) enables me to communicate complex technical architectures with clarity and human warmth.
